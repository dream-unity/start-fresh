On this device

A real language model in your browser. No account or API credit. First use downloads about 1 GB and needs a compatible WebGPU device with roughly 2 GB of free graphics memory.

On your computer

Use the included local server with Ollama. Useful when browser inference is unavailable.

Local setup

Install Ollama, clone this repository, then run:

ollama pull qwen2.5:1.5b
npm start

Open http://localhost:4173. A static hosted copy cannot reach this local server.

Browser speech recognition may send audio to your browser’s speech service. Typed conversation remains available. We never enable your microphone in the background.

What Dream Unity means

Machine holds possibilities. Maker brings attention, choice and action. World brings experience and consequences. Unity connects them through a continuous loop:

Possibility → experience → meaning → intention → action → consequence → revision.

The guide offers interpretations, not measurements of your mind. Question it, correct it, and keep what is useful.
