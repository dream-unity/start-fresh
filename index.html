Browser model

A smaller language model running on your device. First use downloads about 1 GB and needs a compatible WebGPU device with roughly 2 GB of free graphics memory.

Local Ollama model

Use a model already installed on your computer. The default small model has not passed the complete navigation-quality check.

Local model setup

Install Ollama, then run:

ollama pull qwen2.5:1.5b

Use the app’s local server at http://127.0.0.1:4173.

What Dream Unity means

Machine holds possibilities. Maker brings attention, choice and action. World brings experience and consequences. Unity connects them through a continuous loop:

Possibility → experience → meaning → intention → action → consequence → revision.

The guide offers interpretations, not measurements of your mind. Question it, correct it, and keep what is useful.
